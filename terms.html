Terms of Service & Dispensing Policies

Official terms governing prescription verification, medicine authenticity, home delivery thresholds, and branch operational standards across Pakistan.

Home Terms of Service

Official Notice: All pharmaceutical operations comply with the Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan (DRAP) Act and Pharmacy Council protocols.

1. Scope of Services & Acceptance

By accessing the digital services of D. Watson Chemist & Superstore (including our website, digital prescription upload portal, WhatsApp customer care, and branch retail stores), you acknowledge and agree to comply with these terms. D. Watson operates licensed retail pharmacies and multi-department retail superstores throughout Islamabad, Rawalpindi, and northern Pakistan.

2. Prescription Verification & Dispensing Protocol

The health and safety of our patients is paramount:

  • Prescription Requirement: All Schedule-G, prescription-only, antibiotic, psychiatric, and controlled medicines require a valid prescription written by a registered medical practitioner (PMDC / PMC certified).
  • Pharmacist Review: Every uploaded prescription order is thoroughly reviewed by an on-duty Category-A Pharmacist prior to fulfillment to check for dosage, contraindications, and authenticity.
  • 100% Genuine Medicine Guarantee: All pharmaceutical inventory is sourced exclusively and directly from multinational and premier national licensed manufacturers (e.g., GSK, Abbott, Novartis, Pfizer, Getz, Searle). We maintain strict anti-counterfeit protocols.
  • Cold-Chain Integrity: Temperature-sensitive biologicals, insulins, vaccines, and eye drops are continuously stored in calibrated 2°C to 8°C refrigerators equipped with digital temperature monitoring.

3. Operating Hours & Branch Service Policies

D. Watson maintains a clear distinction between round-the-clock emergency hospital coverage and standard retail operations:

  • Headquarters 24/7 Emergency Dispensing: Our historic headquarters and flagship store at F-6 Super Market, Islamabad operates 24/7 emergency medicine dispensing to serve critical patient needs at all hours.
  • Regional Network Hours: Our regional flagship branches (including PWD, Chandni Chowk, G-15, Ghouri Town, Attock, and Gujar Khan) operate during authorized daytime and evening retail hours (typically 08:00 AM to 11:00 PM / 01:00 AM daily).
  • Centralized Universal Helpline: During any hour of the day or night, our central customer helpline at 051-8438111 is active to guide patients to open dispensing stations or coordinate life-saving dispatches.

4. Express Home Delivery Rules & Thresholds

D. Watson provides dedicated rider delivery services for prescriptions, baby care, surgical devices, and cosmetics:

  • Delivery Availability: Available for select flagship branches with designated rider fleets. Availability can be checked on our digital prescription uploader.
  • Delivery Charges: Standard express delivery fee is PKR 200 within designated branch delivery radiuses.
  • Minimum Order Amount: The standard minimum order threshold for home delivery dispatch is PKR 1,000.
  • Free Delivery Privilege: Orders exceeding PKR 3,000 qualify for 100% Free Home Delivery within standard delivery zones.
  • Cold-Chain Transit: Insulin and temperature-sensitive biologicals are dispatched in specialized cold-pack thermal containers.

5. Return & Exchange Safety Regulations

Under official drug safety regulations:

  • Medicines, injections, and refrigerated biologicals cannot be returned or exchanged once they have left the pharmacy or been accepted by the customer, as temperature control and packaging integrity cannot be guaranteed post-handover.
  • Non-medicinal consumer goods, optics, and unopened cosmetic products may be exchanged within 7 days upon presentation of the original computerized sales invoice.
