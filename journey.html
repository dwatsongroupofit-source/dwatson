The Story Behind the Name & Vision

A legacy built on gratitude, uncompromising integrity, and a lifelong commitment to providing genuine pharmaceuticals and world-class healthcare retail to the people of Pakistan.

A Lifelong Tribute to Dr. Watson

The name D. Watson is rooted in an extraordinary moment of heartfelt gratitude. In his youth, Chairman Zafar Iqbal Bakhtawari suffered from a severe eye illness that jeopardized his vision. Seeking medical treatment in England, he came under the compassionate surgical care of Dr. Watson, a renowned British ophthalmologist whose treatment restored Mr. Bakhtawari's eyesight.

Moved by this life-changing experience, the Bakhtawari brothers immortalized Dr. Watson's name as a symbol of authentic medical trust, compassionate care, and unwavering service to humanity.

1978 Genesis & F-6 Super Market

In 1978, three brothers — Chairman Zafar Iqbal Bakhtawari, Co-Chairman Zahid Bakhtawari, and CEO Abid Bakhtawari — established their first retail pharmacy with a pledge: never sell a single medicine whose authenticity could be questioned.

In 1982, the brothers inaugurated their landmark branch in Islamabad's F-6 Super Market. It swiftly became the gold standard for pharmaceutical retail in the federal capital, serving diplomats, heads of state, and families across the region.

Next-Gen Leadership & Expansion

The legacy continues through the dynamic second generation of the Bakhtawari family: Ahsan Zafar Bakhtawari (Managing Director), Bilal Zahid Bakhtawari (Director), and Haris Bakhtawari & Salman Bakhtawari (Directors).

Their leadership has propelled D. Watson across 25+ departmental superstore branches in Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Lahore, and Abbottabad, pioneering cold-chain digital monitoring, computerized optics, luxury cosmetics, and 24/7 express home delivery.

Chronicles of Fifty Years of Growth

Explore the foundational milestones that shaped D. Watson from an independent Islamabad pharmacy into Pakistan's iconic healthcare and retail institution.

1978

The Genesis & Tribute to Dr. Watson

Founded by three brothers: Chairman Zafar Iqbal Bakhtawari, Co-Chairman Zahid Bakhtawari, and CEO Abid Bakhtawari. Named in gratitude to British ophthalmologist Dr. Watson who cured Mr. Bakhtawari's vision in England.

1982

F-6 Super Market Landmark Opening

Inaugurated Islamabad's first modern pharmacy and retail counter in F-6 Super Market, setting the benchmark for authentic medicine dispensing in the capital.

1992

Blue Area Flagship Mega Superstore

Inaugurated the iconic multi-level flagship store on Jinnah Avenue, Blue Area, creating Islamabad's largest integrated departmental healthcare facility.

2005

Pioneering Departmental Superstore Concept

Integrated specialized divisions for international luxury cosmetics, computerized optics clinics, hospital surgical devices, and gourmet hypermarket groceries under one roof.

2018

Stringent Cold-Chain & Pharmacist Certification

Equipped all pharmacy dispensing stations with computerized cold-chain climate monitors (2°C - 8°C) to guarantee 100% potency for life-saving biologicals and vaccines.

2026

Omnichannel Digital Healthcare Hub

Led by founding brothers Chairman Zafar Iqbal Bakhtawari, Co-Chairman Zahid Bakhtawari, and CEO Abid Bakhtawari, alongside the second-generation leadership: Ahsan Zafar Bakhtawari (Managing Director), Bilal Zahid Bakhtawari (Director), and Haris Bakhtawari & Salman Bakhtawari (Directors) — operating 25+ premier branches with 24/7 express home delivery, digital WhatsApp prescription dispensing, and centralized customer helpline (051-8438111).
