Patient Privacy & Data Security

Official Privacy Policy

Your health records, prescription orders, and contact information are protected with the highest standards of pharmaceutical confidentiality and DRAP compliance.

Home Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 | Effective for all D. Watson Chemist & Superstore retail locations, digital platforms, and home delivery services.

1. Commitment to Patient Confidentiality

At D. Watson Chemist & Superstore, safeguarding your health information and personal privacy is a fundamental cornerstone of our 50-year heritage. When you consult our certified pharmacists, present medical prescriptions, or place orders through our website, WhatsApp concierge, or physical retail branches, your data is handled strictly in compliance with the Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan (DRAP) Act and international medical privacy standards.

2. Prescription Records & Medical Order Handling

When you upload or share a prescription via our digital dispensing portal:

  • Prescription images are verified exclusively by licensed Category-A Pharmacists on active duty.
  • Your medical history and prescribed medications are strictly confidential and will never be shared, sold, or rented to third-party marketing agencies.
  • Prescription data is retained only for the legally mandated period required for controlled medicine dispensing records and dosage safety audits.
